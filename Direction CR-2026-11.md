# Calder County — Office of the Regulator

## Direction CR-2026/11

**Issued to:** All programs operating automated resident contact
**In force:** immediately, including in respect of contact already made

---

### 1. The limit

No resident shall receive more than **two contacts in any rolling seven-day period**, counted across all channels.

### 2. Counting

**2.1** A contact is any outbound attempt directed at a resident, whether or not it was delivered, answered, or read. An attempt that fails is a contact.

**2.2** The limit applies **per resident**, not per appointment, per channel, or per contact point.

**2.3** Where a single contact point serves more than one resident, a message directed at one resident counts against that resident only.

**2.4** "Rolling seven days" means any period of seven consecutive days, not a fixed week. A program must be able to demonstrate compliance at any point in time, not merely at week boundaries.

### 3. Retrospective application

**3.1** This direction applies to contact already made. A program must count contact made before the date of this direction when determining whether a further contact is permitted.

### 4. Prioritisation

**4.1** Where the limit prevents a program from contacting a resident about an appointment, the program must record that fact and the appointment concerned.

**4.2** The direction does not permit a program to prefer some residents over others on the basis of any protected characteristic. Where the limit forces a choice between contacts, the basis on which that choice is made must be stated and must be defensible.

### 5. Evidence

**5.1** A program must be able to produce, for any resident and any date, the count of contacts made in the preceding seven days and the basis on which any further contact was permitted or withheld.
